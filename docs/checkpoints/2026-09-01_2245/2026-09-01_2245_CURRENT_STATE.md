## Product State

Lockdin is a Cambridge International AS & A Level revision workspace. An invited user can set a password, sign in, complete five-step onboarding, choose 1–5 subjects, set a global intended exam session with per-subject overrides, browse version-pinned syllabus units/topics/outcomes, record progress and notes, create and complete tasks, log past-paper attempts, view exam dates, and use dashboard, progress, study-plan, calendar, and settings views.

Public self-registration is disabled. `/signup` explains the controlled invite-only beta and sends invited users to the email invitation/password-setup path. The product does not currently provide AI study features, community/social features, external calendar sync, native support tickets, email reminders, or an Information Technology catalogue.
